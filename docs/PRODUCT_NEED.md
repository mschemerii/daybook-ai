# Product Need

Working professionals often begin the day with tasks distributed across memory, notes, messages, and unfinished work. The immediate problem is not always lack of effort; it is the cognitive overhead of reconstructing priorities, blockers, and recent progress before focused work can begin.

Daybook AI addresses this need with one small local workspace for tasks, a daily journal, deterministic focus selection, and optional local-model explanations. The application emphasizes orientation rather than employee measurement. It shows what is due, what is blocked, and which three unblocked items rise to the top under clear rules.

This need is personally useful and relevant to project creator Michael Schemer because it supports organizing responsibilities before beginning a workday while preserving control over private work notes.

Daybook AI is an Ethical AI course prototype. It is not a finished commercial product and does not claim enterprise security, collaboration, integrations, or autonomous operation.
